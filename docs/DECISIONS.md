- **D24** · 2026-11-03 · **Delisting returns and CIK validation** (fixes the D21 "exit flat" bias). A line whose bars end before the research window ends gets a delisting return on the first missing session, classified from the company's own EDGAR filings (365 days before to 120 days after the exit):
  - **continuing** (the CIK is listed later under another line): 0.
  - **SPAC** (SEC SIC 6770, or a blank-check name): 0.
  - **merger** (DEFM14A/PREM14A/DEFM14C/PREM14C/SC 14D9/SC 13E3/425, or an 8-K with Item 5.01): 0.

- **D30** · 2026-11-10 · **P4 tracking-error bound is measured against the execution price, on days with fills.** Approved by the user in conversation.
  - The daily report's holdings model now fills each trade at the open of the 1-minute bar in which its order was submitted (about 09:40 ET), not at the session open. **Execution error** (live − that model) is the P4 bound: under 10 bps on ≥ 90% of days with fills.
